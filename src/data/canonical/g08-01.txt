You do not have to create a digital product from scratch to earn money from one. But the different ways of selling existing digital products give you very different rights.

With affiliate or referral marketing, you recommend someone else's product and earn according to that program's terms. You generally do not own the product.

MRR, PLR, and other licenses can give you permission to resell, edit, rebrand, distribute, or otherwise use a digital product, but those permissions depend on the actual license. The label alone does not tell you what you are allowed to do.

AFFILIATE OR REFERRAL
You recommend another company's product and earn according to its program. You generally do not own the product.

MRR, PLR, AND LICENSING
These can give you permission to resell, modify, rebrand, distribute, or otherwise use existing material, but the exact license determines what is allowed. The acronym does not.

THE DECISION UNDERNEATH THE LABELS
Do you want to recommend a finished product for commission, acquire resale-like rights, or obtain material you can adapt into an offer? Those are different businesses even though each starts with someone else's product.

RIGHTS CHECK
Before paying for an existing digital product, be able to answer:
What exactly am I buying?
What am I allowed to change?
Can I put my brand or name on it?
Can I resell it?
Can my customer resell it?
Where can I sell it?
Can the owner change or revoke the terms?
Am I receiving ownership, or only a license?

If the seller cannot make the rights understandable, that is information worth taking seriously before building around the product.
