WHAT HAPPENS IF THIS BUSINESS WORKS?

Selling an existing digital product can be easy to start precisely because you did not have to create the underlying asset. If sales grow, however, the thing that grows depends on your role.

An affiliate can potentially send many more customers without personally delivering more copies. The creator or merchant absorbs much of the product-delivery load. Your dependence on that merchant grows too. A product change, program closure, commission reduction, tracking failure, or account restriction can affect a business you do not fully control.

With resale or licensing rights, increased sales may create more customer support, payment processing, refunds, file delivery, updates, and other seller responsibilities depending on how the arrangement works. The digital file may be inexpensive to duplicate, but the business around it is not necessarily effort-free.

This is the scalability question for this branch: which parts of the customer experience can grow without your work growing at the same rate, and which important parts remain controlled by somebody else?

WHAT TO VERIFY BEFORE SPENDING MONEY

If you are considering a specific product or rights package, verify the actual license, current seller or affiliate terms, costs, refund rules, permitted sales channels, modification rights, transfer rights, support responsibilities, and any restrictions that matter to your intended use.

Do not buy a license because a sales page says "passive income," "done for you," or "100% profit." Those phrases do not tell you what rights you own, what customers want, or what expenses the business will have.

DO NOT CHOOSE THE ACRONYM FIRST

If you reached this guide because you know you want to sell an existing digital product but have no preference among affiliate, resale, MRR, PLR, or licensing, begin with control.

Do you want to recommend a finished product and let its owner remain the seller? Start with affiliate/referral.

Do you want to become the seller of a product you did not originally create? Explore resale and licensing rights.

Do you specifically want to modify, rebrand, or build from existing material? Explore rights that explicitly permit those actions.

This prevents a common backwards path where someone hears about PLR or MRR first and then searches for any product carrying the acronym.

THE DECISION

Choose based on the role you want. Affiliate/referral keeps you closest to recommending. Resale and licensing move you closer to being the seller and, depending on the rights, may give you more control over the offer.
