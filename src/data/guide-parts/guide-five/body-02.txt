DECISION CHECK
Choose MINE if the appeal is creating an asset your business controls and you are willing to take responsibility for deciding what that asset should be.

Choose SOMEONE ELSE'S if the appeal is starting with a product that already exists and you are comfortable building around another party's product, program, or license.

If your real answer is, "I want someone else to make it, but I still want it to be my product," that can still belong under MINE. Outsourcing creation is not the same as selling someone else's product.

WHAT CHANGES WHEN THE DIGITAL PRODUCT IS YOURS

If you create a guide, template, course, asset, or tool, the customer is buying an offer you control. You decide what problem it solves, what it contains, how it is positioned, and how it evolves.

That control creates creation work. You have to decide what should exist and make sure it is useful enough to buy.

If you sell an existing digital product, much of that creation work has already happened. Your question becomes what relationship gives you permission to participate in the sale and what you actually control under that relationship.
