At this point, you do not need another list of digital product ideas. You need to find out whether your idea can become specific enough to investigate.

Use the prompt below as an interview. ChatGPT should learn about your idea, your customer, your experience, your resources, and your constraints before it starts making recommendations.

BEFORE YOU OPEN CHATGPT
Bring the roughest honest version of the idea you have. "Maybe something about meal planning" is enough. The interview is supposed to create specificity, not require it beforehand.

WHAT A GOOD RESULT LOOKS LIKE
"I should make an ebook" is still incomplete. "A one-off meal-planning guide for night-shift workers who struggle to prepare food around an irregular sleep schedule" gives you a customer, problem, and product direction.

ChatGPT can help expose assumptions, compare formats, organize possibilities, and identify questions. It cannot establish actual demand just because an idea sounds reasonable.

USE THE INTERVIEW UNTIL THE ANSWER CHANGES
The first answer may still be broad. If ChatGPT concludes, "You could create a budgeting guide," ask it to keep interviewing. Who is the guide for? What budgeting situation? What makes their problem different? What should the guide help them accomplish?

Stop when additional narrowing would require evidence you do not yet have rather than more reflection about your own idea.
