WHAT OWNERSHIP MEANS HERE

If you create the product, you control the offer itself. You can decide what it contains, how it changes, how it is packaged, and, subject to any third-party rights or platform rules involved, where it is sold.

That does not mean every component must be created from nothing. You might use licensed fonts, stock assets, software, contractors, or other legitimate inputs. Ownership of the product and personally producing every ingredient are different questions.
