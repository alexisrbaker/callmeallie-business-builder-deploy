START WITH THE JOB, THEN CHOOSE THE FORMAT
Suppose you know how to help someone prepare financially for maternity leave. That knowledge could become a guide, spreadsheet, checklist bundle, course, calculator, or combination. Choosing the format first can force the idea into a shape it does not need.

Ask what the customer needs to get the result. If they need an explanation and plan, a guide may be enough. If they repeatedly organize information, a template may help. If they need demonstrations or practice, structured education may fit. If the value comes from performing a task, a tool may fit.

A direction is worth exploring when there is a plausible connection between a person, a problem or desire, and something digital you could create that helps.
