A one-off sale is exactly what it sounds like: the original purchase does not inherently require the customer to keep paying you.

That does not mean you can only sell to a customer once. Someone might buy one ebook today and another next month, or buy a table from a furniture maker and return later for a bookshelf. Those are repeat customers, but each purchase stands on its own.

One-off products can be attractive because the transaction is simple and the possibilities are broad. Some can also scale extremely well. A digital product, for example, can be sold many times without being recreated for every customer. The tradeoff is that future revenue often depends on finding more customers, giving existing customers another reason to buy, or creating additional products.
