WHAT ONE-OFF DOES NOT MEAN
One-off does not mean cheap or incapable of repeat business. A customer can buy a $5 template once or a $5,000 table once. The same customer may later buy something else. What makes the purchase one-off is that another payment is not required to keep the original purchase complete.

WHY PEOPLE LIKE IT
The transaction can be clear: the customer pays for a defined thing and receives it. Some one-off products, especially digital ones, can be sold repeatedly without recreating them for every buyer.

WHAT TO WATCH
Future revenue does not automatically repeat because a previous customer was happy. It may require new customers, additional products, replacements, upgrades, gifts, or voluntary repeat purchases.

EXAMPLES
A $25 printable planner sold as a finished download is one-off.
A $300 online course sold for one price is one-off even if the customer takes three months to complete it.
A custom dining table is one-off even though the maker may later sell the customer matching chairs.
A $600 course paid in six $100 installments is still one-off if the installments merely pay off one completed purchase.

The question is not how many times money moves. It is whether continued payment is required for continued value.
