WHY THE ADVANTAGES AND DISADVANTAGES EXIST

One-off products can be appealing because the promise is bounded. You can define what the customer receives and complete that promise without an indefinite obligation to keep publishing, supporting, or delivering new value.

The same boundary creates the central challenge. There is no built-in next payment. If finding new customers is expensive or difficult, continually needing new purchases can become important to the economics. A business with several complementary products may have more opportunities for existing customers to buy again, but those are still new purchase decisions.

This is the choice you are making: do you like the idea of selling a complete thing, knowing that future revenue depends on generating another purchase rather than maintaining the original payment relationship?

THE DECISION

One-off sales fit best when you are comfortable earning from distinct purchase decisions rather than requiring the original customer to keep paying for continued access. The next question is what kind of one-off product you would actually want to sell.
